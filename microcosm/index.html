Microcosm

A compact Osmos-inspired example. Absorb smaller cells, dodge larger ones and click to eject mass. Mouse and touch both work.

Mass: 10.0Ready

Move the pointer to steer. Click or tap to eject. Press R to reset.

How the code works

Each cell keeps a position, velocity and mass. Radius is proportional to the square root of mass. The animation loop advances cells, detects collisions, changes mass and renders circles on a canvas. Pause the tab and the simulation pauses too.
